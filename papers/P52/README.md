# The planar GHZ constant for three qubits and the zeros of the Riemann zeta function

**Alexandr Martinevski** · Research preprint · 17 November 2026

- [Published article: 10.5281/zenodo.24293937](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24293937)
- [Publication lineage (Concept DOI): 10.5281/zenodo.24293936](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24293936)
- Published file: `P52_GHZ_ZETA.pdf`

The paper derives **conditional** restrictions on possible globally admissible Bell functionals for planar three-qubit GHZ correlations, using Mellin/Dirichlet series and zero-free regions of the Riemann zeta function. It **does not** prove the Riemann hypothesis, construct an admissible extremal full-face functional, or determine the exact three-party constant.

The main article and proof supplement for the related GHZ inequality results belong to the distinct [P04 publication](../P04/). This directory links to verified public records and does not duplicate or silently modify their released files.
