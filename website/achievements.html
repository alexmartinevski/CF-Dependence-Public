SELECTED SCIENTIFIC RESULTS

Research achievements

Exact extremal theorems, progress on historically studied bounds, and a tightly bounded open problem in planar quantum correlations.

What these claims mean

The results below are stated in released CF Dependence manuscripts. An exact formula inside its stated mathematical class is distinguished from a partial improvement or an unsolved conjecture. Independent proof and historical-priority reviews remain pending; we do not claim that every broader classical problem has been solved.

P04 · GHZExact result in the stated class

Exact planar GHZ threshold for n ≥ 4

The paper derives a closed formula for the optimal local-hidden-variable threshold in the planar XY, full-correlation GHZ scenario, allowing arbitrary finite sets of measurement angles.

κn = (π/2)n ζ(n) / [2(1 + 2−n)ζ(2n)],   n ≥ 4

Example: κ4 = 105/34,   vXY(GHZ4) = 34/105 ≈ 0.32380952.

Unlike finite-setting numerical estimates, this is an expression for the full continuum-of-settings optimization in the specified full-correlator model. The earlier 2024 work by Designolle, Vértesi and Pokutta supplied rigorous bounds rather than this general closed formula.

Scope: planar XY measurements and full correlations. This is not a claim about every Bell scenario or arbitrary measurements. Independent verification of the proof and of historical priority has not yet been completed.

P04 · GHZ3Strict bounds; exact value remains open

Three-party GHZ: a narrow interval and an exact-value conjecture

For three parties the sharp value is not yet proved. The published paper provides a new finite-harmonic Bell lower bound and an analytical upper bound from an explicit square-wave/Möbius local model.

2.034968153548… ≤ κ3 ≤ 105ζ(3)/(2π3) ≈ 2.035329429153

0.49132095555… ≤ vXY(GHZ3) ≤ 0.49140818163…

These two visibility bounds improve both ends of the interval 0.49129–0.49143 reported by Designolle, Vértesi and Pokutta in 2024. The remaining κ₃ interval is only about 0.01775% of its upper bound.

Conjecture: the upper bound is exact, κ₃ = 105ζ(3)/(2π³). This equality has not been proved.

The lower bound uses an analytic reduction followed by 272 reproducible rational inequalities in the K7 certificate. This is computer-assisted proof, not an independent external proof-review.

The remaining dual Bell-functional construction encounters arithmetic oscillations linked to the Möbius function and zeros of the Riemann zeta function. This does not make the conjecture equivalent to the Riemann hypothesis.

P04 · PROBABILITYExact result in the stated class

Optimal covariance constant under strong mixing

For bounded complex random variables measurable with respect to two σ-fields, P04 establishes the sharp coefficient in the covariance inequality with the classical strong-mixing coefficient α.

|Cov(X,Y)| ≤ 4√2 · α(ℱ,𝒢) · ‖X‖∞ · ‖Y‖∞

The literature contains Dvoretzky's historical bound 2π, discussed in Bradley–Bryc (1985). The constant 4√2 is smaller and is claimed sharp in P04, with an equality construction; for real scalars the constant is 4.

This comparison uses the strong-mixing coefficient and the same bounded-observable normalization. A complete search for equivalent earlier sharp results and an independent proof review are still outstanding, so no first-ever priority is claimed.

P04 · 1985Partial response in a restricted class

Sharper multilinear estimates and the question from 1985

Bradley and Bryc observed in 1985 that their general multilinear coefficients were not optimal. P04 derives sharp real and complex factors 2ⁿ and 2ⁿκₙ for a natural class of multilinear forms real-valued on real arguments.

cnreal = 2n;   cncomplex = 2nκn

Important distinction: the 1985 result allowed general complex multilinear forms, whereas the sharp factors in P04 concern a real-on-real subclass. Consequently the old problem is not solved in its full generality. The n=3 complex sharp factor also remains open.

P01 · MATHEMATICSExact result in the stated class

Universal characteristic-function dependence profile

An extremum over joint laws of bounded complex random variables is reduced to a formula in the prescribed moduli of their means. This unifies several characteristic-function factorization problems.

𝒞d(r1,…,rd) = R − cos(min{Σj arccos rj, π}),   R = ∏jrj
Cd = max0≤θ≤π/d (cosdθ − cos(dθ))

The universal constant has explicit values C₂=1, C₃=2/√3, C₄=9/7. Extremal constructions use a common fair sign in the corresponding regime; C_d increases to 2. This is a proven exact profile, not a claim that an independently documented century-old open question was solved.

P13 · MATHEMATICSExact result in the stated class

Sharp quantitative stability near extremal dependence

P13 strengthens the sharp inequality into a stability theorem: if the dependence defect is close to its maximum, the joint distribution must be close to the entire family of equality cases.

distWp(P,ℰd) ≤ Kd,p Δ1/(2p)

For d≥3, any finite p≥1 and sufficiently small deficit Δ, the displayed Wasserstein exponent is optimal, even after optimizing coordinate phases. A structured discrepancy separating location and mass errors admits an optimal square-root rate.

The theorem is highlighted for its exact exponent and extremal geometry, not as an independently verified solution of an older externally posed question.
